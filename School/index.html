About SR Academy

SR Academy offers a modern and well-maintained campus designed to support effective learning and development.

Our classrooms are spacious, well-ventilated, and equipped with smart boards to make lessons more interactive and engaging.

The school features a fully equipped computer lab with the latest systems and high-speed internet, helping students develop strong technical skills.

In addition, the campus includes well-designed science laboratories, a rich library, and clean, safe surroundings that create an ideal learning environment for every student.

SR Academy provides safe and reliable bus transportation for students from various parts of the city.

All school buses are well-maintained, equipped with GPS tracking, and operated by trained drivers and attendants to ensure safety and punctuality.

Each vehicle follows strict safety guidelines and is regularly inspected to provide a comfortable and secure travel experience for our students.

Our transport service covers major routes, making it easy and convenient for parents and students to commute to and from school.

SR Academy promotes sports excellence along with academics. Students actively participate in volleyball and football with great enthusiasm.

Our volleyball team has won national championships four times. Trained coaches and modern facilities help players improve their skills.

The football team has achieved several national titles. Skilled coaches guide students to build teamwork and sportsmanship.

Players are selected through trials and regular practice. Many represent the school at national tournaments and win awards.

SR Academy organizes various cultural and entertainment events throughout the year to encourage student participation and enjoyment.

One of the main highlights is the annual function, Barshik Utsab, where students showcase their talents through music, dance, and drama performances.

The school also hosts special evenings featuring famous singers and live musical performances, creating memorable experiences for students and guests.

Fun-filled DJ nights and celebration events are organized to promote joy, togetherness, and a vibrant school atmosphere.
