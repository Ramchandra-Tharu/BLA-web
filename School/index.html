About SR Academy

Established in 2003, SR Academy has consistently been at the forefront of academic excellence and holistic student development. Our mission is to provide a nurturing environment where students can thrive both academically and personally, preparing them to face the challenges of the modern world with confidence and integrity.

At SR Academy, we believe that education is not limited to textbooks. We focus on fostering creativity, critical thinking, and leadership skills among our students. Our highly qualified faculty members are committed to providing personalized attention to each student, ensuring that every learner reaches their full potential.

The school is equipped with state-of-the-art infrastructure, including modern classrooms, science and computer laboratories, a well-stocked library, and extensive sports facilities. We encourage students to participate in extracurricular activities such as arts, music, debates, and sports to build confidence and teamwork skills.

Over the years, SR Academy has achieved numerous accolades in academics, sports, and cultural events at regional and national levels. Our alumni continue to excel in various fields, reflecting the quality education and guidance they received during their formative years at the school.

Our vision is to create a community of lifelong learners who are socially responsible, globally aware, and equipped with the skills necessary to succeed in an ever-changing world. At SR Academy, every student’s journey is valued, and we strive to instill a passion for learning that lasts a lifetime.
