About M.D

Sirzan Chaudhary is the founder of SR Academy, established in 2003, with a vision to provide quality education and all-round development for students. Under his leadership, the academy has grown into a reputed institution known for its academic excellence and sports achievements. His dedication, hard work, and commitment to nurturing young minds have made SR Academy a place where learning meets inspiration.F

Mr. Chaudhary believes in discipline, innovation, and holistic growth. He guides SR Academy toward success by fostering creativity and leadership. His vision is to shape students into responsible and confident individuals.
